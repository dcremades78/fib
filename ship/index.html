01 · The Opportunity

SHIP already has the audience. Time to give them the thread that connects it all.

A curated line-up, a UNESCO city as your venue, and a growing international reputation. But all that info lives scattered across the website, social media and printed maps. An official app brings it together — and works even when the old town loses signal.

Signal lost in stone walls

Šibenik's medieval fortresses, tunnels and narrow streets are stunning — but they block mobile signal. VIKIN's offline-first app keeps showing the line-up, map and schedule when the network can't keep up.

Six venues across the city

From the Deck at St. Michael's Fortress to the underground Tunel, through Hull, Arsen, Azimut and Portside — artists are scattered across town. A personal agenda with clash alerts means no one misses their favourite act.

Showcase + conference, all in one

SHIP is more than a music festival — it's a showcase and industry conference. Attendees need one single hub for sets, panels, afterparties and city navigation, not five different sources.

A rising festival deserves its own digital layer

Featured in Rolling Stone UK, The Line of Best Fit and Clash — SHIP is building a name in international festival culture. A branded app that attendees keep from one edition to the next.

04 · The VIKIN Difference
Offline-first and
anonymous. At a
festival, that's everything

No signal, no problem

Line-up, map, agenda and navigation all work downloaded. We deliver updates while reducing data and battery consumption.

No sign-up, no data

Attendees open the app without creating an account. We don't collect personal data; all analytics are aggregate and respectful.

Communication without algorithms

What you publish reaches everyone who has the app, without a social network deciding what gets seen and what doesn't.
