The audience

Three days inside the venue

Viña Rock brings tens of thousands of people to Villarrobledo every day, and many of them spend the whole weekend camping. The more time they spend at the venue, the more times they open the app.

60,000 people every day

This is the organisers' forecast for the 30th edition. We use it to build three scenarios of app use, and you can adjust the one that fits best.

29 April – 1 May 2027

Three festival days and a welcome party on the 28th. A 30th edition gets more attention and more interest from brands.

Camping and the full weekend

The idea

Help first. Then sponsor.

A brand works in an app when it solves something at the right moment. These are the six rules we use to design every format.

Useful before visibleEvery format helps with something: finding your tent, drinking water, taking the bus back.
Right time, right placeWhat shows on the screen changes with the time of day and the area of the venue.
Everything sponsored is labelledAnd for alcohol brands: responsible messages and nothing aimed at minors.
Few pushes, with a limitA few alerts in the right place. If they annoy people, the channel is lost.
Never about emergenciesSafety alerts are never mixed with any brand.
No sign-up, no personal dataMetrics are aggregated and anonymous.

The 60,000 attendees per day is the organisers' forecast. The rest are starting estimates, and real ticket data and real app use will make them more accurate. If you change the attendees or the app use, the scenario becomes yours.

How each impact is calculated
Different peopleAttendees per day × festival days ÷ days each person comes. App usersDifferent people × % who use the app. App opensUsers × opens per user per day × festival days. Slot on HomeFestival opens × (days of the slot ÷ festival days). If there are more brands than slots, the slots are shared and each brand gets less. Highlighted pinMap views × pin visibility × number of pins. Each pin counts separately. Utility sponsorshipUses of the module (for example “Find my tent”). Sponsored postUsers × reach of a post × number of posts. Sponsored pushUsers × % with notifications × number of pushes. Before and afterEach campaign is one post and one push outside the festival days. InteractionsTaps on the slot on Home and on pins, and opens of posts and pushes. CPMPrice ÷ (impressions ÷ 1,000). It is what the brand pays for every 1,000 times it is seen. Equivalent valueImpressions × reference CPM ÷ 1,000. It is what the same exposure would cost in digital advertising, using the reference CPM that you set.

Ideas to celebrate it inside the app

Ideas to think about together. Each one opens a new format for a brand, and almost all of them are built to order †.

30th anniversary passport

One stamp per stage and per brand activation. When it is complete, a prize draw or anniversary merch with a sponsor behind it. For the draw, sign-up is optional and with consent.

My Viña Rock, personal summary

At the end, each person gets their own summary: saved concerts, stages, hours of music. It is made to be calculated on the phone, with no personal data, and to be shared on social media with the name of a brand.

Countdown and line-up in phases

The app can be installed months before. A countdown with a sponsor and the line-up revealed in phases, so people open it long before the festival.

30 years, 30 moments

A timeline inside the app with posters, photos and memories from every edition, presented by a brand. It is built with material that the festival already has.
